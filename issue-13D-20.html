National conference recap and Touch and Try

A January 2027 demonstration day at the Capitol is being planned.

More on Plans and regulation

What was said

A national legislators conference was reviewed. Speaker 1 noted heavy attendance and high costs, and that future travel plans include conferences in Atlanta.

A January demonstration day is being planned. The “Touch and Try” day for lawmakers would be at the state capitol complex in January 2027.

The debate is about what to show. The group weighed bringing utility hardware, such as bucket trucks, against interactive technology displays that could attract key lawmakers.

Space is a limit. Large fleet vehicles may not fit the venue.

Why it matters

My reading

Lawmakers who touch the equipment and talk with crews remember the work. A display that fits the space and draws legislators from key energy committees beats a large vehicle that cannot be parked.

Say it in plain words

A Touch and Try day is planned for January 2027 at the Capitol. It lets lawmakers see and handle utility equipment. The open question is whether to bring bucket trucks or build interactive displays, and a venue that can handle large vehicles. The plan is to design exhibits that attract the legislators who sit on energy committees.

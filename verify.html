A suggested order for study

  1. Start with the difference between energy and capacity in the reliability section.
  2. Use the dependable capacity tool until the gap makes sense to you.
  3. Read the PJM and MISO tables, then try the price cap model.
  4. Work through the data center section, keeping contracts and new plants apart.
  5. Scan the race board and read the strategy section for timing.
  6. Read each script aloud once, then take the quiz.

Leadership speculation and caucus governance

One possible leader may be receding, and a right hand model for energy was floated. Unconfirmed.

More on People and organization

What was said

Putting people in positions is a gamble. Speaker 1 said that even a strong President places people in roles without being sure how they will do.

One expected leader may be receding. Speaker 1 had expected Sutherland to step up but heard the day before that Sutherland may be receding.

Early optimism faded. Speaker 1 recalled a pattern where early year hope turned into frustration at not meeting goals.

A right hand model was floated. One member suggested leadership of Stoddem plus a right hand person to guide energy. Speaker 3 doubted that is enough.

Overview

Built July–August 2026 as the evidence base for the Political R&D concept — a proposed formalized legislative-foresight capability for Ameren Illinois' government relations function. The concept was pitched in a mentor meeting with Kristol Simms (VP, Clean Energy Transition), based on the idea of proactive, long-horizon (5–10 year) issue scanning.

The dataset

A 385-bill dataset spanning 20 subject areas from 2020 to 2026, drawn from a multi-session legislative archive analysis that categorized 300+ tracked bills across six Illinois General Assembly sessions to identify recurring policy themes. Operation Resilient Grid served as the founding proof case for the concept.

Scope note: this tracks Illinois state-level bills only, not national legislation. The Illinois General Assembly introduces roughly 6,000+ bills per session in total; the 792-bill portfolio referenced elsewhere is the filtered energy/utility subset of that total, not a national count.

Where it landed

Two work products came out of the review: a comprehensive evidence base and a lean six-page executive white paper aimed at Patrick Smith, Chad, and Betsey Evans.

After testing several framings, the conclusion (September 2026) was that a new formal Political R&D function isn't needed — the existing GR model (legislator relationships, coalition and peer-utility engagement, internal operational signals, and national/state trend-watching) already covers the need. The white paper instead documents current practice and proposes two small tweaks: quarterly internal listening check-ins with other business units, and a statutory-deadline tracking calendar.

The white paper deck includes a market-comparison slide on Tier 2 legislative-intelligence platforms (FiscalNote/PolicyNote, Quorum, State Net) as a point of reference — not because Ameren plans to buy one, but because their national and multi-state breadth is a reasonable guide to which out-of-state ideas could migrate into Illinois. Tier 1 (large consulting firms) was ruled not relevant to this question, and Tier 3 (relationship and political-risk intelligence) is what the GR function already does day to day.

More on People and organization

What was said

The annual retreat moved south. Speaker 1 attended the Energy Association's retreat, held in Clayton this year instead of its usual northern locations such as Chicago or Naperville.

Reactions split by region. Attendees from the Chicago area were unhappy with the change. Regular attendees made the trip, but some top executives did not attend.

A lobbyists event is coming. A contract lobbyists event runs from October 15 to 16, and Speaker 2 will attend the dinner at Porter's on October 15.

Why it matters

My reading

Where an event is held changes who shows up. If top northern leaders skip southern events, the people with decision power miss the conversation. Plan the location with that in mind.

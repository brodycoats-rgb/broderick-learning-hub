Coal retirements and carbon capture

Powerton got an extension, some Edwards and Elmwood units end by 2027, and Prairie State and Dallman run to 2045.

More on Plans and regulation

What was said

Retirement timelines were reviewed. The speaker went through statutory dates and those set by the letter process for Illinois coal plants.

Powerton got an extension. It was granted under the formal letter writing process.

Edwards and Elmwood are set to close. Remaining units are scheduled to decommission by December 31, 2027.

Prairie State and Dallman run to 2045. Both received operating extensions through 2045. Prairie State faces ongoing economic strain.

A university is testing carbon capture. The University of Illinois is running an on site pilot at Prairie State that captures carbon and releases it right away, not storing it deep underground.

The decision was to track filings. The group will follow compliance filings through the 2027 closure deadlines.

Why it matters

My reading

This is the map of which coal units are staying and which are leaving, and it frames the replacement question. A pilot that captures carbon without storing it is a research step, not a commercial answer, so the economics of units running under extensions remain unclear.

Say it in plain words

The coal map in short. Powerton got an extension. The remaining units at Edwards and Elmwood are set to close by the end of 2027. Prairie State and Dallman have extensions through 2045, and Prairie State is under economic strain. A university pilot at Prairie State is testing carbon capture, but it releases the carbon again, so it is a test and not a commercial solution.

Capture versus storage

Capturing carbon separates it from exhaust. Storing it means placing it underground for the long term. A pilot that only captures tests the first step. The expensive and permitted part, storage, is a separate step.

Terms on this page

CEJA Background
Illinois's Climate and Equitable Jobs Act. It sets retirement requirements for certain fossil units, including some in environmental justice communities.

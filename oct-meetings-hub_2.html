The five big ideas to carry into any room

  1. Reliability is now a political story, not only an engineering one.
  2. Large new load and shrinking firm supply are colliding at the same time.
  3. Where authority sits (legislature, ICC, IEPA, RTO) decides what is possible.
  4. Timing and sequencing matter as much as substance, especially around the veto session.
  5. Plain, fact based messaging beats strategy heavy messaging with a general audience.

Post election politics and labor demands

Labor may seek unemployment benefits for strikers, and cost is a concern.

More on Labor, Bears, pensions

What was said

Labor has made its priorities clear. Speaker 2 reported on post election expectations and said organized labor was very direct about what it wants.

The big ask is benefits during a strike. Labor wants the right to collect state unemployment benefits while on strike.

It is a cost worry. Speaker 2 called it a significant expense that the company cannot afford to take on.

The politics point toward concessions. Speaker 2 expects successful Democrats to give labor concessions after the election, which makes the issue worth watching across other states.

Why it matters

My reading

If strikers can draw unemployment, a strike costs workers less, and the employer's bargaining position changes. For a utility, the cost shows up in negotiating leverage and in the tax rate employers pay into the benefit fund.

Say it in plain words

Labor made its priorities clear. One of the biggest is the ability to collect state unemployment benefits during a strike. That would change the cost and leverage of a strike, and the speaker called it a major expense. Because successful Democrats are expected to give labor concessions after the election, we should track what other states have done and watch for a bill here.

What other states do

A small number of states already allow some benefits for striking workers, with waiting periods and limits that differ from state to state. Check the current list before you say which ones, because laws in this area have been moving.

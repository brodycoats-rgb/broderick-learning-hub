Coal ash rules and the IMA conference

The conference runs October 19 and 20. Members with coal ash exposure will be contacted.

More on Plans and regulation

What was said

The IMA energy conference is coming. The Illinois Manufacturers' Association Energy Conference is on Tuesday, October 20, 2026, with an opening reception on Monday, October 19.

IEPA is pursuing coal ash rules. The agency is working on compliance requirements for coal ash storage and remediation.

Members with exposure will be contacted. The group will directly contact member companies with financial exposure to coordinate unified advocacy. The decision was to host the reception and reach out to coal ash permit holders before rule negotiations start.

The cost risk is high. The proposed rules could impose high liability and cleanup costs.

Why it matters

My reading

Coal ash rules are expensive and long lived. Companies that speak together before the agency writes its draft have more influence than those who comment afterward. The conference is also a chance to meet industrial customers who care about energy cost.

Say it in plain words

The Illinois Manufacturers' Association energy conference is on October 20, with a reception the night before. At the same time, the state environmental agency is moving on coal ash rules, which could be very costly. The plan is to host the reception, meet industrial customers, and contact member companies with coal ash exposure so that the industry speaks with one voice before negotiations begin.

What coal ash rules usually cover

They often address how ash ponds are closed, how groundwater is monitored, and how long a company must care for a site after closure. Each choice affects cost, so the exact words are worth reading.

Short clips were mentioned. Speaker 1 referred to brief video clips of less than a second, with positive participant engagement.

Why it matters

My reading

Writing down forecasts and scoring them later is how a team learns which instincts are reliable. It also guards against remembering only the calls that came true.

Say it in plain words

Speaker 1 is tracking everyone's election projections so they can be compared with results. The suggestion is to settle on a clear format and a regular schedule for recording them, so the team can learn which calls hold up.

A simple scoring habit

Write the call, the confidence and the date. After the result, mark right or wrong and note why. After a few rounds you will know who is good at which kinds of calls. This habit is my suggestion.

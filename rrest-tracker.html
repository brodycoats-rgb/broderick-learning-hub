Purpose

The RREST (Resource Reliability & Engagement Strategy) tracker is the working internal document behind Operation Resilient Grid — Ameren Illinois' campaign to retain five natural gas combustion turbine (CTG) facilities facing CEJA-mandated retirements: Venice, Goose Creek, Pinckneyville, Raccoon Creek, and Kinmundy. Together the five facilities represent 2,092 MW of capacity.

The tracker organizes the case for retention — reliability rationale, stakeholder engagement, and legislative context — into ten working sections, built out over roughly three months and kept current through the campaign.

Cross-functional coordination

The tracker is maintained alongside weekly cross-functional coordination calls that keep legal, regulatory, and GR teams aligned on messaging and status as the retention case develops.

Supporting work products

  • An SME subject-routing tool covering 109 topics across 25 sections, used to route incoming questions to the right internal expert.
  • A BIPA (Bipolar Illinois Power Agency proceedings / bill-specific) executive briefing prepared for Patrick Smith.
  • Strategy documents applying Kingdon's Multiple Streams Framework and other policy-analysis frameworks to the retention question.
  • Legislative tracking and data-center bill analysis materials prepared for Senate and House hearings.

See also: the fiscal impact analysis and the legislator power matrix, both part of the same campaign.

Earlier foundational work

Before the RREST tracker itself, early-2026 groundwork included SharePoint dashboards, PowerPoint decks, meeting debrief documents, and weekly regulatory-update scripts, which fed into the current tracker structure.

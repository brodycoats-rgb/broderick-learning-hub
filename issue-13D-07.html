Association staffing and December event

A senior vice president is being hired, and a multi state gathering is set for December in Palm Springs.

More on People and organization

What was said

The association is changing its staff. A long serving colleague retired, and a new senior vice president is being brought in. That person will get a chance to interact with the members.

A large December gathering is coming. The association will host the other state energy associations for a full week in December in Palm Springs, and those groups work closely with this one.

Two approvals were recorded. The notes say the hiring process for the new senior vice president was approved and the week long event was confirmed.

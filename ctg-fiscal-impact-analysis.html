Overview

This analysis quantifies the local property tax payments made by Ameren's five CTG facilities (Venice, Goose Creek, Pinckneyville, Raccoon Creek, and Kinmundy) to the local governments and school districts in their communities, to support the case for retention under Operation Resilient Grid.

Key figures

MetricAmount
Combined annual property tax payments (five facilities)~$2.93M
Cumulative payments to date$45.3M+

The analysis also translates facility-level economic contribution into job-loss equivalents, used in legislative leave-behind materials to make the local economic-development case concrete for members whose districts include these communities.

How it's used

These figures support meetings and leave-behinds with legislators representing the five CTG communities, and feed the broader retention case tracked in the RREST tracker.

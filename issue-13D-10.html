CEJA mitigation plan and the IRP

Import forecasts look high and about 800 MW of Constellation upgrades may be double counted. The IRP is public the day before the veto session.

More on Plans and regulation

What was said

The CEJA mitigation plan and the IRP are close. The group discussed both, and a release of the IRP is expected soon.

The models show a lot of imports. Forecasts had out of state power imports tripling or quadrupling over five to ten years.

About 800 MW may be counted twice. The state counted about 800 MW of upgrades from Constellation, but PJM saw that most of those megawatts are already spoken for. The speaker called it a lot of confusion.

The IRP will appear the day before the veto session. The working group gets no advance copy, and the legislature is not expected to act right away.

The decision was to prepare now. The group will build an immediate response analysis for the day the IRP is public.

Why it matters

My reading

If the plan counts megawatts that are already committed, the shortfall is larger than it looks. And a document that appears the day before the veto session leaves almost no time to respond. Preparing a checklist ahead of time is the practical answer.

Say it in plain words

The state plan shows imports growing three to four times over the next five to ten years, and it may count about eight hundred megawatts of Constellation upgrades that PJM says are mostly already committed. The plan will be public the day before the veto session, with no preview. So the working group will prepare its response now, so that it is ready the day the document appears.

A short checklist for the day the IRP is released

Terms on this page

IRP Background
Integrated Resource Plan. A long range plan for meeting demand with supply. The notes expect IRP findings in November.
CEJA Background
Illinois's Climate and Equitable Jobs Act. It sets retirement requirements for certain fossil units, including some in environmental justice communities.
Uprate Background
An upgrade that raises a plant's output.

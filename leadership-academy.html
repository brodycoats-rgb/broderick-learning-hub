What to listen for

  • Pace: roughly 120 to 160 words a minute sounds conversational. Faster than about 170 is hard to follow, slower than 100 can drag. These are rules of thumb.
  • Fillers: um, uh, like, you know. A few are normal. Aim to cut the count in half over a month.
  • Hedges: I think, maybe, just, sorry. Keep them for real uncertainty.
  • Open and close: point in the first ten seconds, ask in the last ten.
  • Pauses: stop for one second after the key point. It feels long to you and sounds confident to them.

Operating stance

  • Lead the issue, not the room. Bring a recommendation with every problem.
  • Keep every commitment. Credibility is the currency of influence without authority.
  • Know who decides, when, and what is possible before that date.
  • Name the source and trust level of every number.
  • Say what you can confirm, and what you cannot.

What the research and practice say

  • Development sticks when it is practiced on real work with feedback, not when it is a one time event.
  • Rehearsing hard conversations before having them is a widely recommended use of AI coaching, alongside human coaches who bring judgment and personal connection. Vendor claims about return on investment are largely unsupported, so treat them with care.
  • Public service guidance on leading without formal authority stresses taking ownership, using data, acting without being asked, keeping commitments and building relationships.

Self assessment

Rate where you are today and where you want to be in 90 days. 1 is new to it, 3 is solid, 5 is the person others come to. Be honest, then ask someone else to rate you on the same scale.

How your answers guide your focus

  1. Gap x weight. For each competency, gap is target minus now. It is multiplied by a weight for how much the skill matters to your current work. The biggest products rank first.
  2. Top three become your focus. They feed the 90 day plan and the Academy learning path.
  3. Strengths. Ratings of 4 or 5 are listed so you can use them as leverage while you practice weaker areas.
  4. Blind spots. Optionally add how others would rate you. A difference of 2 or more is flagged for a conversation.
  5. Next three moves. Each focus area links to the Academy modules and a first drill.

Honest ratings matter more than high ones. Rate against what leadership needs, not what you have done before.

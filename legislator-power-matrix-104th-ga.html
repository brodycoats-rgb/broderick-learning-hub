Operation Resilient Grid

Overview

A relationship and influence-mapping tool built for the 104th Illinois General Assembly, developed as part of Operation Resilient Grid to identify which members and committee positions matter most to the CTG retention campaign and to Ameren Illinois' broader legislative agenda.

How it's used

The matrix informs meeting prioritization, coalition-building, and outreach sequencing alongside the RREST tracker and the Grid Outreach Legislator Conversation Guide.

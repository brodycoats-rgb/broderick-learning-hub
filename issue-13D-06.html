Why it matters

My reading

A settlement tells you the parties agreed to terms, but the commission still decides. Until it does, the details are not final. The satisfaction number matters only if the method behind it holds up.

What a settlement does and does not do

In a rate case, parties often negotiate a settlement and present it to the commission. The commission can approve it, change it, or reject it. Until it acts, treat the numbers as proposals.

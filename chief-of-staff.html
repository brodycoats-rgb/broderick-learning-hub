                          Setting up the live feed (once)

                          1. Upload refresh-cos.yml to .github/workflows/ in your repo, next to refresh-news.yml.
                          2. Open the repo, go to Actions, choose "Refresh strategy desk feeds" and press Run workflow once. After that it runs about every 30 minutes.
                          3. Reload this page. The tabs fill in and the strip at the top shows Ameren.

                          Until then, the News desk offers search links and the Markets tab links to quote pages.

                          Limits

                          • Headlines come from Google News search and publisher feeds, so they include opinion and syndicated items. Open the story before you rely on it.
                          • Investor relations: SEC filings are included. Ameren press releases may not have a public feed I could confirm, so use the investor relations site for those.
                          • District detail, Missouri election specifics and the Illinois pipeline in MW are not filled from public sources. Add them from your own knowledge.
                          • Nothing here is legal advice. Counsel clears anything that touches a pending case.

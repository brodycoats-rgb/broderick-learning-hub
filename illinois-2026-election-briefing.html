At a glance

Democrats are favored in every Illinois statewide race. The close contests are a handful of legislative seats, one congressional district, and the argument over electricity prices and data centers.

How to use this page

  • Open the purple script box at the top of each section for a plain-language briefing you can say out loud, plus the key points.
  • Click any underlined name, race tile or candidate card to open a short profile with background, positions and risks.
  • Green boxes are suggested follow-ups. They are recommendations, not facts.
